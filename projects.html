Behind the scenes

THE THINKING BEHIND THE BUILD

Why I built it

A ranked backlog can tell you what comes first without explaining why. I wanted to make the reasoning behind a roadmap visible—and open to challenge.

My contribution

Problem framing, prioritisation logic, interaction design and AI-assisted prototyping.

What I want to learn next

Whether the prototype helps PMs spot missing evidence, reconsider priorities and explain their decisions more clearly.

The choices behind it

Transparent scoring
Make the criteria visible so people can question the ranking.
Adjustable constraints
Show how a change in capacity affects the roadmap.
Explicit trade-offs
Explain what gets deferred when something else takes priority.
Human judgement
Support the PM’s reasoning while leaving the final decision with them.

Built with AI assistance. The decision challenger is rule-based, with no live AI. Usage and customer outcomes have not yet been measured.
